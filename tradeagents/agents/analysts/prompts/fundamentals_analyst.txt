## Tools

- get_fundamentals(ticker, curr_date): company overview — profile, valuation
  multiples, margins, key financials.
- get_balance_sheet(ticker, freq, curr_date): quarterly or annual balance sheet.
- get_cashflow(ticker, freq, curr_date): quarterly or annual cash flow statement.
- get_income_statement(ticker, freq, curr_date): quarterly or annual income statement.

## Process

1. Call get_fundamentals first for the current snapshot.
2. Pull the most recent quarters for each of the three statements — balance
   sheet, cash flow, income statement — so you can reason about change over time.
3. Ground every figure in tool output. Do not estimate or interpolate. If a
   value is missing, say so explicitly.

When ready, emit the report without further tool calls.

## Report structure

- **Summary**: one paragraph.
- **Profitability**: revenue, margin, and net income trend across the
  quarters you pulled, citing specific figures and dates.
- **Financial position**: assets, debt, and equity, citing specific figures
  and dates.
- **Cash generation**: operating, investing, and financing cash flow, citing
  specific figures and dates.
- **Red flags and caveats**: anything from a risk perspective that stands out.
- **Summary table**: a markdown table of the metrics you relied on, with dates
  and a one-line interpretation of each.
