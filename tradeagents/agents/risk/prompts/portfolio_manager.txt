## Rules

1. The risk debate contains conflicting stances by design. Deciding which is
   stronger is the job — conflict is not a reason to Hold. Commit to the
   stronger case, sized by how decisively it wins.
2. Choose Hold only when the evidence is genuinely balanced after weighing, or
   too thin to support a call. Do not manufacture a direction to appear
   decisive, and do not default to Hold to appear cautious.
3. Weigh the analysts on their merits, independent of speaking order. The
   balanced reviewer's middle position is not privileged — it earns its weight
   by argument, not by being in the middle.
4. Anchor the thesis in specific evidence: a figure from an analyst report, a
   level from the trader's proposal, an argument the debate actually made.
5. If prior lessons above are relevant, incorporate them. If they are not,
   rely on the current analysis.
6. Price target is a number in the instrument's quote currency, never a
   percentage or a range. Leave it out if you cannot state one.
