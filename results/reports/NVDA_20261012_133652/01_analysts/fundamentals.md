# Fundamental Analysis: NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA)
**Analysis Date:** 2026-10-08

### Summary
NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) continues to demonstrate exceptional financial performance, driven primarily by sustained, explosive demand for its AI-focused semiconductor solutions. As of October 2026, the company exhibits industry-leading growth rates, robust margins, and massive cash generation capabilities. While the firm's financial position is exceptionally strong in absolute terms, the rapid expansion of receivables and heavy reliance on specific high-growth market segments present areas for investor scrutiny.

### Profitability
NVIDIA’s income statement displays a remarkable and consistent growth trajectory over the last five quarters. Total revenue grew from $46.74 billion in the quarter ended 2025-07-31 to $96.22 billion in the quarter ended 2026-07-31. This growth has translated efficiently to the bottom line, with quarterly Net Income increasing from $26.42 billion to $59.69 billion over the same period. Operating margins remain highly attractive, reflecting the company’s strong pricing power and competitive dominance in the AI infrastructure market.

### Financial Position
As of 2026-07-31, NVIDIA reports Total Assets of $320.27 billion, significantly up from $140.74 billion on 2025-07-31. This asset growth is supported by a strong Equity base of $228.98 billion. Total Debt stands at $38.35 billion. While the debt-to-equity ratio reported is 16.97, this figure must be viewed in the context of the company's substantial cash and short-term investment holdings ($62.47 billion as of 2026-07-31), indicating a highly liquid position. The company maintains a healthy current ratio of 4.59.

### Cash Generation
NVIDIA’s cash flow generation remains potent. Operating Cash Flow (OCF) for the quarter ended 2026-07-31 was $24.08 billion, down from the peak of $50.34 billion in 2026-04-30, primarily due to large shifts in working capital, specifically the increase in accounts receivable. Investing cash flow reflects aggressive management of liquidity, with significant funds directed into investment securities. Financing cash flow is dominated by substantial share repurchases, totaling $19.73 billion for the quarter ended 2026-07-31, illustrating a commitment to returning capital to shareholders.

### Red Flags and Caveats
1.  **Receivables Growth:** Accounts receivable increased dramatically to $63.06 billion as of 2026-07-31, rising from $27.81 billion a year prior. While this aligns with top-line growth, a faster rate of growth in receivables than revenue could potentially signal extended payment terms or credit risk.
2.  **Market Expectations:** The Forward PEG ratio of 0.28 suggests the market anticipates extremely high future earnings growth. Any moderation in the pace of AI infrastructure spending could result in significant valuation re-ratings.
3.  **Concentration Risk:** Revenue is highly concentrated in data center and AI-related applications, making the company disproportionately sensitive to capital expenditure cycles within the hyperscale cloud and AI enterprise sectors.
